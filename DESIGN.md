# FoodBridge Design System

## Overview

FoodBridge is the steady neighbor of social-impact software: warm, competent, and unhurried. It carries two very different jobs on one visual language — a public site that has to earn trust from restaurant owners, supermarket managers, volunteers, and families in need, and a four-role operational workspace where people move food quickly without confusion. The interface should read as **credible infrastructure with a human hand**: closer to a well-run community organization than to a startup landing page, and nowhere near a cartoon.

The personality is expressed through restraint plus one repeated signature. Neutrals do 80–90% of the work: white canvas, a light gray-green well (`{colors.background-subtle}`), warm charcoal text (`{colors.foreground}`), and a single 1px hairline (`{colors.border}`). Forest green (`{colors.primary}`, stepped to `{colors.primary-strong}` wherever a solid fill carries a white label) is the one brand voice, used for the mark, active navigation, key metrics, chart series, and the primary action on each screen. The second green — the pale tint `{colors.secondary}` — is the system's "permission" surface: it marks anything approved, active, matched, or safe to act on. Amber and deep red appear only as status, never as decoration.

The signature is a **route line**: a 1px dashed connector with small circular nodes that threads the three-step flow (donor → volunteer → family), the delivery progress bar, and the 404 state. It is the one graphic motif allowed to repeat, and it always carries meaning — it is always a path from surplus to a plate.

**Key characteristics**

- Forest green single accent, pale-green tint as the second voice, warm charcoal text; no second brand hue.
- Deliberately soft geometry: 16px cards, 22px media frames, 10px controls, fully round pills and icon buttons.
- Layered, low-opacity, **green-tinted** shadows rather than neutral black drop shadows, so elevation feels organic on white.
- Public pages are photography-forward and airy (80–96px section rhythm); dashboards are calmer and denser (24px content rhythm) with hairline-separated tables.
- Self-contained system typography — no webfont requests, no CDN, no `@import`. Visual identity comes from weight, tracking, and spacing discipline, not from a downloaded face.
- Photography is always framed, never bled edge-to-edge behind body copy; scrims exist only where text must sit on an image.
- Motion is functional: 150–220ms state confirmation, 2px hover lift on cards, no entrance choreography in the working canvas.

**What to avoid**

- Purple/indigo gradients, glassmorphism, neon accents, or a second saturated brand color.
- Cartoon vegetables, hand-drawn illustration sets, emoji as icons, or confetti in the operational UI.
- Corporate-cold gray-blue palettes, dense 12px admin tables, and hard 0px corners.
- Full-bleed image carousels, autoplaying video, or decorative blob/wave SVG backgrounds.
- Marketing copy inside dashboards; dashboards use operational language ("12 pickups today", not "Amazing impact!").

## Colors

The palette is a **green-led neutral system**: white and light gray-green build the surfaces, deep forest green carries identity and action, and three status hues appear only as small badges and notifications.

- **Primary forest green (`{colors.primary}`, #218C5A):** the brand voice. Logo mark, active nav text, KPI accents, chart series 1, icon chips, focus ring. It is a deep, slightly blue-leaning green that reads as institutional rather than minty.
- **Primary strong (`{colors.primary-strong}`, #1B7A4E):** the AA-safe twin of the brand green, used for solid fills that carry white text and for green text below 18.5px. White on #218C5A measures 4.2:1; white on #1B7A4E measures 5.3:1. At fill scale the two are visually indistinguishable, so **solid buttons, active pills with white labels, and small green links use `primary-strong`**, while the brand token `primary` remains the identity color for graphics, large numerals, and icons.
- **Primary deep (`{colors.primary-deep}`, #14603D):** pressed states, headings on tinted panels, and the dark CTA band. Reaches 7.6:1 on white.
- **Secondary pale green (`{colors.secondary}`, #E8F5EC):** tinted section bands, chips, badges, table hover, icon tiles, approved states. It is a surface, not an accent — large areas of it are correct.
- **Accent amber (`{colors.accent}`, #C77C0A):** the "urgent / expiring soon" hue. Used as a dot, icon, or 12px label — never as button fill (white on amber is only 3.3:1) and never as body text. Text on amber tint uses `{colors.accent-foreground}` (#8A5305, 5.8:1).
- **Destructive red (`{colors.destructive}`, #B42318):** destructive buttons, rejected states, validation. Tint `{colors.destructive-subtle}` with text `{colors.destructive-text}` for badges (7.9:1).
- **Neutrals:** canvas `{colors.background}` (#FFFFFF), content well `{colors.background-subtle}` (#F6F8F7), card `{colors.surface}`, text `{colors.foreground}` (#16211C, 16.5:1 on white), secondary text `{colors.muted-foreground}` (#5C6B63, 5.6:1 on white, 5.3:1 on #F6F8F7), tertiary metadata `{colors.foreground-subtle}` (#64736B, 5:1 on white, 4.7:1 on #F6F8F7 — 12px and up only, never for sentences).
- **Borders:** `{colors.border}` (#E3EAE5) is the workhorse 1px hairline; `{colors.border-strong}` (#CBD8D0) is for control edges, dashed drop zones, and hover borders. Never use #000 at low opacity — the border and shadow system is green-tinted (`rgba(22, 33, 28, …)`).

**Surface ladder.** Page canvas is white on public pages and `{colors.background-subtle}` inside dashboards; cards are always pure white; tinted bands are `{colors.secondary}`; hover fills step to `{colors.surface-hover}` (#F2F7F4). Never stack more than three surface values in one card region.

**Accent discipline.** On any single viewport the saturated green may appear at most twice as a solid fill (one primary CTA + one active navigation state). Every other green expression is tint or hairline. Status hues must stay under 5% of pixels. This cap is what keeps a four-role application from turning into a color-coded toy.

**Image overlays.** `{colors.overlay}` (rgba(22,33,28,0.45)) dims images behind floating cards; `{colors.scrim}` (rgba(10,34,23,0.72)) is the minimum for white text on photography. **Contrast contract.** Body text is ≥4.5:1, large text (≥18.5px semibold or ≥24px regular) ≥3:1, UI borders and icons ≥3:1, focus ring ≥3:1 against both the control and the surrounding surface. Any new color must be checked before use; `foreground-subtle` never carries sentences.

## Typography

The type system is **one family, three weights, tight tracking**. There is no webfont: the stack is `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`. This is a deliberate product decision — the application must render identically offline, on slow networks, and inside low-bandwidth field conditions where volunteers use it on mobile data. Identity comes from rhythm: semibold headings with negative tracking against generous body leading.

- **Announce (600):** display, h1–h4, buttons, metric numerals, table overline labels.
- **Emphasize (500):** navigation, field labels, card titles at small sizes, chips, sidebar items.
- **Read (400):** all body copy, table cells, helper text, captions.
- **Never 700+.** Bold-on-bold is the fastest way to make this system look like a generic dashboard. Never italicise headings; never use justified text.

**Rules.** Uppercase appears only at `overline` scale with ≥0.06em tracking. Display and h1 use `text-wrap: balance`; body uses `text-wrap: pretty`. Body line length stays 50–75ch (`max-width: 65ch`). Numerals in KPIs, tables, quantities, weights, distances, dates, and chart axes always use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Metric numbers never sit next to a unit at the same size — units are 60% size in `{colors.muted-foreground}` with a 4px gap ("1,284" + "kg"). Public page headings use sentence case; product confirmations use sentence case with a period only in full sentences. No more than three type sizes above the fold on any public page.

## Layout

**Grid.** A 12-column fluid grid inside a 1240px container. Gutters 24px desktop, 16px tablet and mobile. Page padding: 32px ≥1024px, 24px 640–1023px, 16px <640px. Dashboard content is capped at 1240px and left-aligned within the content well, never centered in a way that leaves an asymmetric gap under the sidebar.

**Spacing scale.** 4px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96 (`spacing.1` … `spacing.24`). Section rhythm on public pages is 80–96px desktop / 48px tablet / 40px mobile. Card inner padding is 24px desktop / 20px mobile; compact cards and table cells use 16px. Stack spacing inside a card: 4px between a label and its value, 8px between metadata rows, 16px between content groups, 24px between card sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is **layered and green-tinted**, never a single black drop shadow. Because the page sits on white and light gray-green, elevation is communicated by a stack of two or three very low-opacity shadows in `rgba(22, 33, 28, …)` plus a 1px border. Cards at rest feel placed on the page, not floating above it.

| Level | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `shadow.none` | `none` | Table rows, list items, footer, page sections |
| `shadow.xs` | `0 1px 2px rgba(22,33,28,0.05)` | Cards at rest (with 1px `{colors.border}`), inputs, chips |
| `shadow.sm` | `0 1px 2px rgba(22,33,28,0.04), 0 2px 8px rgba(22,33,28,0.05)` | Card hover, dropdown menus, sticky public nav after scroll |
| `shadow.md` | `0 2px 4px rgba(22,33,28,0.04), 0 8px 24px rgba(22,33,28,0.07)` | Sticky sidebar panels, floating hero card, popovers, toasts, chart tooltips |
| `shadow.lg` | `0 4px 8px rgba(22,33,28,0.05), 0 20px 48px rgba(22,33,28,0.10)` | Dialogs, mobile drawer, command palette |
| `shadow.xl` | `0 24px 64px rgba(22,33,28,0.18)` | Only for a modal above another modal (rare) |

**Rules.** Only one elevation step per interaction: hover promotes `xs → sm` (or `sm → md`) with `translateY(-2px)`; pressed returns to the base shadow and `translateY(0)`. No inner shadows, no glows, no colored shadows. Dialogs and drawers pair `shadow.lg` with scrim `{colors.overlay}` and never with a border. Sticky surfaces (public nav, dashboard top bar, table headers) separate from content with a 1px border rather than a shadow until content passes beneath them. Focus is elevation-independent: `outline: 2px solid {colors.ring}; outline-offset: 2px` on `:focus-visible`, with an offset color of white on green fills.

**Motion for elevation.** 150ms for color/shadow-only transitions, 180ms for card lift, 200ms for dropdown and toast entry, 220ms for drawer and dialog entry. Easing `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, remove all translate/scale transforms and keep opacity/color crossfades.

## Shapes

Geometry is **soft but disciplined**: containers are generously rounded, controls are gently rounded, and only true circles are fully round. Nothing in the system is square-cornered, and nothing is pill-shaped unless it is a badge, chip, or single-line pill button.

| Token | Value | Applied to |
|---|---|---|
| `{rounded.xs}` | 6px | Tag pills, thumbnails inside tables, tiny chart legend swatches |
| `{rounded.sm}` | 8px | Logo mark, checkboxes, tooltips, menu item hover |
| `{rounded.md}` | 10px | Text inputs, selects, textareas, buttons, sidebar items, icon chips |
| `{rounded.lg}` | 14px | Inner media frames, toasts, mobile record cards, alert banners |
| `{rounded.xl}` | 16px | Cards, table containers, KPI cards, upload zones, modals' inner sections |
| `{rounded.xxl}` | 22px | Hero media, large feature panels, dialogs, the CTA band |
| `{rounded.full}` | 9999px | Status badges, filter chips, avatars, icon buttons, progress bars |

**Borders.** 1px everywhere; 2px only for a selected-state ring (role cards, selected filter, dragged file target). Dividers are `{colors.border}` at 1px — no 2px section rules, no colored left rails on cards. A green left rail is allowed only inside the sidebar for a pinned/primary destination, never on a content card.

**Route line (signature motif).** A 1px dashed stroke (`{colors.border-strong}` at rest, `{colors.primary}` for the active segment), `stroke-dasharray: 4 6`, `stroke-linecap: round`, with 8px filled circular nodes in `{colors.surface}` and a 2px `{colors.primary}` stroke. It connects three or four nodes horizontally on desktop and vertically on mobile, with node labels beneath at `{typography.label-sm}`. It appears in the how-it-works section, the volunteer delivery progress (active segment solid `{colors.primary}` 2px), the empty-state illustration slot, and the 404. It is never used as a decorative background pattern.

**Photography framing.** Hero: 16:9, `{rounded.xxl}`, `object-fit: cover`, no border, `shadow.sm`, plus a 1px inset ring `rgba(22,33,28,0.06)` to separate white photo edges from the white canvas. Donation cards: 4:3 at `{rounded.lg}` inside a `{rounded.xl}` card, top corners matched to the card, flush to the card's top and side edges with no internal padding. About/impact narratives: 3:2 at `{rounded.xl}`. Avatars: 1:1 at `{rounded.full}`, 32/40/56px. Images never sit inside a bordered box with a visible gap on all sides, and body copy never overlays a photo without a scrim.

**Hero overlay treatment.** On the CTA band and any full-bleed image section, apply a single directional gradient scrim `linear-gradient(100deg, rgba(10,34,23,0.86) 0%, rgba(10,34,23,0.55) 45%, rgba(10,34,23,0.12) 100%)` over the image, with text held in the first 55% of the width. On the home hero, the image is a framed panel rather than a background: text lives in the left column, the photo sits right, and a single floating white mini-card (`shadow.md`, `{rounded.lg}`, 14px padding) overlaps the photo's bottom-left corner by 24px showing the most recent rescue ("Bakery surplus · 12 kg · 2 km away"). Mobile: the mini-card collapses to a single non-floating row beneath the image.

**Visual fallback tile.** Any category without photography renders a 4:3 tile in `{colors.secondary}` with a centered 40px line icon at 1.75px stroke in `{colors.primary}` and the category name in `{typography.overline}` uppercase below it at 12px/0.08em, `{colors.secondary-foreground}`, 8px gap. Never use a gradient, striped placeholder, or an empty gray box.

**Iconography.** Monoline, 1.75px stroke, rounded caps and joins, 24px grid, currentColor. Sizes 16/20/24/32. Icons never carry color of their own except inside an icon chip. Filled icons are reserved for the notification bell's active state and star ratings. Do not mix icon families; do not use emoji as icons.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep the page mostly neutral — white canvas, `{colors.background-subtle}` well, `{colors.secondary}` tint — and let forest green mark only action and status.
- Do use `{colors.primary-strong}` for any solid green fill that carries white text and for green text below 18.5px; reserve `{colors.primary}` for the mark, icons, large numerals, and chart series.
- Do keep every body text pair at ≥4.5:1 and every interactive target at ≥44×44px, including icon buttons, table row actions, and inline close buttons.
- Do pair every status with text and a dot — never color alone — and use the five mapped status tokens rather than inventing new greens or ambers.
- Do hold the 4px spacing scale and the 24px card padding; put 4px between a label and value, 8px between metadata rows, 24px between card sections.
- Do use `shadow.xs` at rest and step exactly one level on hover with a 2px lift in 180ms.
- Do frame photography (16:9 hero, 4:3 cards, 3:2 narrative) inside `{rounded.xxl}` / `{rounded.lg}` / `{rounded.xl}` containers with `object-fit: cover`, a descriptive `alt`, and the icon-only fallback tile when no image exists.
- Do stack tables into labeled record cards below 900px and turn the sidebar into a focus-trapped drawer below 1024px.
- Do write operational microcopy in dashboards ("3 pickups due before 18:00") and mission-level copy on public pages — the register shifts between the two, so no single headline should serve both.
- Don't introduce a second saturated brand color, purple/indigo gradients, neon accents, or glassmorphism.
- Don't use emoji as icons, cartoon vegetable illustrations, decorative blobs/waves, or confetti inside the operational UI.
- Don't put a colored left rail on content cards, add shadows to table rows, or exceed one elevation step per interaction.
- Don't use font weights above 600, uppercase labels without ≥0.06em tracking, or display text without negative tracking.
- Don't set body copy over photography without a scrim that proves 4.5:1 against the lightest pixel beneath it.
- Don't animate layout, trap focus outside a dialog, or leave a spinner running without a timeout and an escalation path.
- Don't let a status color appear as a button fill: amber and red are signals, not actions, except the single confirming destructive button inside a dialog.
